# Sessions, 25 August to Halloween 2026

Twenty nights, Tuesday and Thursday at 5.30, from tonight to the Thursday before Halloween. Three themes: starting, stopping, and the first half of landing.

Everything here is the athletic development station only, seventeen minutes, run three times as the groups rotate. The four parts of every night and the kit are in the [activity bank](activities.md). This page is what changes from night to night.

## The two kinds of night

Every Tuesday is a push night. Three days after the last match, four days before the next one, so this is where the teaching happens and where the pace goes up.

Every Thursday is an ease off night. There is a match two days later, every week. Keep the wake up game and the movement, drop the challenge game, and send them home fresher than they arrived.

Nothing new is ever taught on a Thursday.

## Sessions 1 to 8, starting

The four parts are the same all block and they are written out in the [activity bank](activities.md#theme-1-starting): jog and sprint to the nearest cone, first step work in waves of six, the sit on their own disc, and the lying start race. The body shape all block is **the sit**.

### 1. Tuesday 25 August, hurling

First night at the station, so spend two minutes on the layout before anything else. Six lanes, a disc each, and where they stand when they are not running. Do that properly once and you never do it again.

- **Movement.** Standing starts and starts from a walk, nothing else. Six goes each, slow. Show the first step going forward and low.
- **The sit.** Find it. Feet under the shoulders, heels flat, chest up. Ten slow ones, five seconds each.
- **Coach one thing.** Knees pointing the same way as the toes. Pick three boys, fix one thing each.

### 2. Thursday 27 August, football

Match in two days. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** The same two starts, ball in hand, at match pace.
- **The sit.** Ten slow ones, then hold it while you walk round trying to make them laugh.
- **Coach one thing.** The first step goes forward, not up. Boys who bounce upright first are wasting a step.

### 3. Tuesday 1 September, football

- **Movement.** Add the crouch start and the start from one knee. Four starts now, and they choose nothing, you call it.
- **The sit.** Ten slow ones, then five held for ten seconds.
- **Coach one thing.** Heels staying flat on the ground.

### 4. Thursday 3 September, hurling

Match in two days. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** The four starts with a hurl in hand, at pace.
- **The sit.** Sit and stand ten times, slow on the way down, hurl across the shoulders.
- **Coach one thing.** A hurl in one hand does not mean the other arm stops working.

### 5. Tuesday 8 September, hurling

- **Movement.** Add sitting on the ground and lying on the front. All six starts now, two goes at each.
- **The sit.** Arms straight overhead. Harder than it sounds and it shows you who is stiff.
- **Coach one thing.** Getting off the ground in one movement rather than crawling up.

### 6. Thursday 10 September, football

Match in two days. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** All six starts at pace, ball in hand.
- **The sit.** Hold it while a partner gently nudges a shoulder. Ten seconds, then swap.
- **Coach one thing.** Head up coming out of the start.

### 7. Tuesday 15 September, football

The hardest night of the block. Push it.

- **Movement.** Starts on a shout, so they do not know when it is coming. Then the same with eyes shut.
- **The sit.** On one leg, a hand on a partner for balance. Five each leg.
- **Coach one thing.** Reacting without falling out of the start. If a boy is falling over, put him back to standing starts.

### 8. Thursday 17 September, hurling

Match in two days, and the last night of the theme. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** All six starts with a hurl, one go at each, at pace. No teaching, just let them go.
- **The sit.** Called out at random during the wake up game. Freeze where they are and hold.
- **Coach one thing.** Nothing. Watch, and ask three boys to name the shape.

## Sessions 9 to 16, stopping

The most important theme in the bank. The four parts are in the [activity bank](activities.md#theme-2-stopping): traffic lights, run and stop on a line, the bow, and stop on the number. The body shape all block is **the bow**.

The fault you are hunting for eight nights is stopping upright with straight legs. It should look like sitting down into the stop.

### 9. Tuesday 22 September, hurling

New theme, so this is a teaching night before it is a pushing one.

- **Movement.** Walk it, then jog it. Two steps to stop and no more. Say out loud that the stop is the sit they already know.
- **The bow.** Find it. Bend from the hips, back flat, knees soft. A hurl held along the back tells them when the back is flat.
- **Coach one thing.** Straight legs at the stop. Stop the lane and show it again if you see it.

### 10. Thursday 24 September, football

Match in two days. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** Jog building to a run, ball in hand, stop on the line.
- **The bow.** Ten slow ones, hold five seconds each.
- **Coach one thing.** Count the steps out loud. Two, not five.

### 11. Tuesday 29 September, football

- **Movement.** Build to a proper run. Move the stopping line further out so they arrive faster.
- **The bow.** Bow, then sit, then stand. Link the two shapes so they feel that one runs into the other.
- **Coach one thing.** Chest forward and backside back.

### 12. Thursday 1 October, hurling

Match in two days. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** At pace with a hurl. Hold up fingers as they run and they shout the number at the stop.
- **The bow.** On one leg, a hand on a partner. Five each leg.
- **Coach one thing.** Head up while stopping. That is the whole reason for the numbers.

### 13. Tuesday 6 October, hurling

- **Movement.** Stop, hold for two seconds, then go again. Then the same with no hold, stop and straight back into a sprint.
- **The bow.** Hold fifteen seconds, three times.
- **Coach one thing.** The start out of the stop. Most boys stand fully upright first and waste it.

### 14. Thursday 8 October, football

Match in two days. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** Stop on the number at pace, ball in hand.
- **The bow.** Bow and reach down to touch the disc, back stays flat the whole way.
- **Coach one thing.** Falling forward past the line.

### 15. Tuesday 13 October, football

The hardest night of the block. Push it.

- **Movement.** Stopping on one foot. Walk it, then jog it, then run it, and only move them on when it looks right.
- **The bow.** On one leg, arms out sideways, ten seconds each leg.
- **Coach one thing.** Knees falling inwards on the one foot stop. If it looks bad, put him back to two feet. No argument.

### 16. Thursday 15 October, hurling

Match in two days, and the last night of the theme. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** Run, stop, turn, go. Hurl in hand, at pace.
- **The bow.** Thirty seconds of the bow, then thirty of the sit. Two shapes they should now be able to name.
- **Coach one thing.** Nothing new. Ask three boys what the two shapes are called.

## Sessions 17 to 20, landing

Four nights of the eight fall before Halloween. The rest run to 12 November. The four parts are in the [activity bank](activities.md#theme-3-landing): silent bunny hops, jumping and landing over mini hurdles, the landing shape, and the longest silent jump. The body shape all block is **the landing**.

Noise and knees falling inwards. Those two things are the whole theme.

### 17. Tuesday 20 October, hurling

New theme, so teach it before you push it. No hurdles tonight.

- **Movement.** Jump on the spot, land silent, freeze for two seconds. Then a small jump forward and the same freeze.
- **The landing.** Jump from their own disc, land, freeze. Walk round and gently nudge shoulders. If they wobble they were not really holding it.
- **Coach one thing.** Noise. If you can hear him land, he landed badly. Say it once and then just keep pointing at the loud ones.

### 18. Thursday 22 October, football

Match in two days. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** Mini hurdles, one per lane, two feet to two feet. Over, land silent, hold for two seconds.
- **The landing.** Land and hold five seconds, ball in hand.
- **Coach one thing.** Knees falling inwards on the landing.

### 19. Tuesday 27 October, football

- **Movement.** One foot to two feet, then two feet to one foot. Slowly, and hold every single landing.
- **The landing.** One foot landings, five each leg, hold three seconds.
- **Coach one thing.** The one foot landing is where the knee goes. Watch it closely and put anyone who looks bad back onto two feet.

### 20. Thursday 29 October, hurling

Match in two days, on Halloween. Ease off, drop the challenge game.

- **Movement.** Hurdles at pace, hurl in hand, two feet to two feet, land and hold.
- **The landing.** Three shapes named now. The sit, the bow and the landing, thirty seconds each.
- **Coach one thing.** Quiet landings. They will be wound up for Halloween, so hold the line on silence and finish on time.

## Things that will change this

**A night gets cancelled.** Push everything back a session. The theme takes eight nights, not four weeks, so a lost Thursday means the theme finishes on the following Tuesday and everything after it slides. Do not skip a night to catch up.

**A fixture moves.** The code of a night follows the match. Thursday is the code playing that Saturday, Tuesday is the code that played last Saturday. If the fixture list changes, swap the night's code and leave the content alone. The theme does not care which code it is.

**It is lashing rain.** Everything here works in the wet except lying or kneeling on the ground. The lying starts become crouch starts and the ground work becomes standing work. The wet night swaps are at the end of the [activity bank](activities.md#wet-night-alternatives).

**A group turns up late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes, because that is the part they get nowhere else all week.
