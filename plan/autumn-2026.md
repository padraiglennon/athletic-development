# Sessions, 25 August to Halloween 2026

Twenty nights, Tuesday and Thursday at 5.30, from tonight to the Thursday before Halloween. Three themes: starting, stopping, and the first half of landing.

Everything here is the athletic development station only, seventeen minutes, run three times as the groups rotate.

Every night has its own run sheet in [sessions](sessions/), one folder per theme, a single page with the layout, the four parts, the progression and what to coach. Take that page to the pitch. This page is the calendar and the reason the blocks are in this order. The activities themselves are in the [activity bank](activities.md).

## The two kinds of night

Every Tuesday is a push night. Three days after the last match, four days before the next one, so this is where the teaching happens and where the pace goes up.

Every Thursday is an ease off night. There is a match two days later, every week. Keep the wake up game and the movement, drop the challenge game, and send them home fresher than they arrived.

Nothing new is ever taught on a Thursday.

## Sessions 1 to 8, starting

The four parts are the same all block and they are written out in the [activity bank](activities.md#theme-1-starting): jog and sprint to the nearest cone, first step work in waves of six, the sit on their own disc, and the lying start race. The body shape all block is **the sit**.

The block builds by adding starts. Two on the first night, four by session 3, all six by session 5. Session 7 is the hardest night, where the start goes on a shout and then with eyes shut. Session 8 teaches nothing, it just watches.

## Sessions 9 to 16, stopping

The most important theme in the bank. The four parts are in the [activity bank](activities.md#theme-2-stopping): traffic lights, run and stop on a line, the bow, and stop on the number. The body shape all block is **the bow**.

The fault you are hunting for eight nights is stopping upright with straight legs. It should look like sitting down into the stop.

The block builds by speed first and then by difficulty. Walking and jogging on session 9, a proper run in by session 11, stop and go again on session 13, and one foot stops on session 15, which is the hardest night of the year and the one to be strictest about.

## Sessions 17 to 20, landing

Four nights of the eight fall before Halloween. The rest run to 12 November. The four parts are in the [activity bank](activities.md#theme-3-landing): silent bunny hops, jumping and landing over mini hurdles, the landing shape, and the longest silent jump. The body shape all block is **the landing**.

Noise and knees falling inwards. Those two things are the whole theme.

Nothing gets jumped over on session 17. The hurdles come out on session 18, once the landing is quiet on the spot, and session 19 is the first time they land on one foot.

## Things that will change this

**A night gets cancelled.** Push everything back a session, and take the next run sheet in order rather than the one with tonight's date on it. The theme takes eight nights, not four weeks, so a lost Thursday means the theme finishes on the following Tuesday and everything after it slides. Do not skip a night to catch up.

**A fixture moves.** The code of a night follows the match. Thursday is the code playing that Saturday, Tuesday is the code that played last Saturday. If the fixture list changes, swap the night's code and leave the content alone. The theme does not care which code it is.

**It is lashing rain.** Everything here works in the wet except lying or kneeling on the ground. The lying starts become crouch starts and the ground work becomes standing work. The wet night swaps are at the end of the [activity bank](activities.md#wet-night-alternatives).

**A group turns up late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes, because that is the part they get nowhere else all week.
