# Session 12, Thursday 1 October

| Next match | Hurling, Saturday 3 October |

Match on Saturday, so the stop at match pace with a hurl, and their heads up while they do it.

## On the ground

- Twelve cones: six lanes with the stop line at 10 metres
- 25 marker discs beside the lanes, one per boy
- A hurl each

![Six lanes with a stop line at 10 metres and five metres beyond it to run off, and a marker disc for each boy beside the lanes](../diagrams/stop-line.svg)

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

## Splitting them up

- Four boys to a lane, one behind the other behind the start cone. Spare boys go to the shortest lane.
- The boy at the front of each lane runs. Six lanes, so six boys go together on your whistle. That is what a wave means below.
- The cone in the middle of the lane is the stop line. The five metres past it is room to run off if he cannot stop, not somewhere to keep running.
- After his go he jogs back up the outside of his lane and joins the back of it.

## Structure and rules

**0 to 2, wake up game: traffic lights.**

They run about inside the grid. "Green" is run, "red" is stop dead and hold still. Anyone still moving after red does five sits on the spot. Six or seven goes, and make some of the greens short.

**2 to 9, movement: stopping with a hurl, head up.**

Hurl carried down at his side in the hand he uses in a match, free arm swinging.

Four waves stopping on the line as normal. Then start holding up fingers as they run in, and they shout the number back at the stop. That forces the head up, which is what happens in a match, and it is much harder than it sounds.

**9 to 15, body shape: the bow.**

The bow on one leg. One foot on the disc, the other leg straight out behind him as he bows forward, a hand on a partner's shoulder for balance.

Five each leg, five seconds each. Backs stay flat and the hips stay level. Most of them will wobble.

**15 to 17, finish.**

No challenge game tonight. Walk them in, ask two or three of them to name the shape, and hand them to the next station on time.

Rules, the same every night:

- Nobody starts before the whistle, and one coach whistles for all six lanes.
- A boy who has run jogs back up the outside of his lane.
- Every boy stays on his own disc for the shapes.
- Same station for all three groups. Set it up once, run it three times.

## Progression

Work down this and stop wherever the group is. Getting to the bottom is not the aim.

1. Run in with a hurl, stop on the line. Four goes.
2. The same with fingers held up as they run, shouting the number at the stop. Six goes.

## Coaching points

- **Coach one thing tonight: head up while stopping.** That is the whole reason for the numbers.
- A hurl held along the back is still the quickest way to show a flat back.
- Ease off night. Match in two days.

## If it is not working

- **Running late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes.
- **More than four waiting in a lane.** Add a seventh lane. Boys in a queue learn nothing.
- **It looks bad.** Go back a step and slow it down.
- **Lashing rain.** Wet hurls are slippy. Check grips before each wave and drop the one leg bow if they cannot stand up in it.
