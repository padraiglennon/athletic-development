Thursday 29 October · Jumping

Ease off nightCode HurlingTheme JumpingBody shape The landingNext match Hurling, Saturday 31 October

Last night before Halloween and the last night of the autumn. All five shapes get named.

On the ground

  • 12 cones, six lanes
  • 6 mini hurdles, 15 cm
  • 25 discs, a hurl each

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

0 to 2Wake up game: copy the jump

  • Pairs, spread through the grid. One boy makes up a jump and lands it.
  • His partner copies it exactly, landing and all. Then they swap.
  • Two minutes. Tell them a jump nobody can land quietly does not count.

2 to 9Movement: hurdles at match pace

  • Waves of six over the hurdle with a hurl in hand.
  • Each boy picks his own landing, two feet or one. Six waves.
  • Stand at the side and listen. Say nothing.

9 to 15Body shape: all five shapes

  • A disc each. Call them in order and hold each for thirty seconds: the sit, the plank, the big step, the bow, the landing.
  • No demonstration. They have had four nights on each of them.
  • Then call them again in a mixed up order, ten seconds each.

15 to 17Finish: name all five

  • Walk them in.
  • Ask the group: "Name the five shapes." Then ask one boy to say what each one is for.
  • That is the autumn finished. Tell them so.
Coach one thing: Nothing. Find out how many of the five shapes they can name.
  • How many boys can name all five without help. That number is the honest score for the autumn.
Lashing rain. Two feet landings only, and the shapes on the ground become the sit and the bow.
Land quietly, with the knees bent and the knees apart. U11 athletic development · 17 minutes · 25 boys · run three times
