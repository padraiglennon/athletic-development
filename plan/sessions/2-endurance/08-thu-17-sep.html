Thursday 17 September · Endurance

Ease off nightCode HurlingTheme EnduranceBody shape The plankNext match Hurling, Saturday 19 September

Last night of the theme and a match in two days. Watch, do not teach.

On the ground

  • 10 cones, one loop about 25 by 12 metres
  • 25 discs inside the loop
  • A hurl each

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

0 to 2Wake up game: shadow jog in threes

  • Threes, one behind the other, jogging anywhere inside the loop.
  • The leader picks the pace and the direction and the other two copy him exactly.
  • Change the leader every forty seconds. Tell them the leader may not sprint.

2 to 9Movement: the loop with a hurl

  • Two goes of ninety seconds at talking pace with a hurl in hand.
  • Two minutes walking between, and the walk is part of it.
  • Stand in one place and let them come past you. Say nothing.

9 to 15Body shape: the plank

  • In pairs. One holds the plank, the other counts out loud to twenty and watches his back.
  • Swap. Three each.
  • The counter has one job: "Is his backside up or down?"

15 to 17Finish: who could still talk

  • Walk them in.
  • Ask three boys: "On the second run, could you still talk?"
  • Tell them the theme changes on Tuesday and it is sprinting.
Coach one thing: Nothing. Find out who has learned the pace.
  • A boy who now paces himself without being told. That is the theme having worked.
Lashing rain. The plank becomes the sit, held for twenty seconds.
Keep running at a pace he can hold, and know what that pace feels like. U11 athletic development · 17 minutes · 25 boys · run three times
