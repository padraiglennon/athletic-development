# Session 8, Thursday 17 September

Hurling · Ease off night. Match pace, then send them home fresher · Theme: Endurance · Next match: Hurling, Saturday 19 September

Last night of the theme and a match in two days. Watch, do not teach.

## On the ground

- 10 cones, one loop about 25 by 12 metres
- A hurl each

![One loop about 70 metres round, everybody running the same way](../diagrams/loop-grid.svg)

![Talking pace, and the question that checks it](../diagrams/move-pace.svg)

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

## The seventeen minutes

**0 to 2, wake up game: shadow jog in threes.**

- Threes, one behind the other, jogging anywhere inside the loop.
- The leader picks the pace and the direction and the other two copy him exactly.
- Change the leader every forty seconds. Tell them the leader may not sprint.

**2 to 11, movement: the loop with a hurl.**

- Three goes of ninety seconds at talking pace with a hurl in hand.
- Two minutes walking between, and the walk is part of it.
- Stand in one place and let them come past you. Say nothing.

**11 to 17, finish: who could still talk.**

- One last go of sixty seconds at talking pace, then stop.
- Walk them in.
- Ask three boys: "On the second run, could you still talk?"
- Tell them the theme changes on Tuesday and it is sprinting.

## Coach one thing

**Nothing. Find out who has learned the pace.**

- A boy who now paces himself without being told. That is the theme having worked.

**Lashing rain.** No change. Nothing tonight goes on the ground.
