First night, so two minutes go on showing them the layout: their lane, their disc, and where they stand when they are not running. Take those two minutes out of the wake up game, never out of the body shapes.

## On the ground

- Twelve cones, six lanes, 15 metres long
- 25 marker discs beside the lanes, one per boy
- No hurls tonight

![Six lanes 15 metres long, a cone at each end, and a marker disc for each boy beside the lanes](../diagrams/lanes-15m.svg)

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

## Splitting them up

- Four boys to a lane, standing one behind the other behind the start cone. Spare boys go to the shortest lane.
- The boy at the front of each lane runs. Six lanes, so six boys go together on your whistle. That is what "a wave" means below.
- After his run he jogs back up the outside of his lane and joins the back of it. He is up again in about twenty seconds.

## Structure and rules

**0 to 2, wake up game: sprint to the nearest cone.**

They jog about inside the grid, hands empty. On "cone", every boy sprints to the nearest of the twelve cones and stops dead beside it, then back to jogging. Four or five goes.

They will all run to the same cone first time. "The nearest one to you, not the one your friend is going to."

**2 to 8, movement: the first step.**

Two starts tonight and nothing else.

*Standing start.* Feet a shoulder width apart, one foot half a step in front, knees soft. On the whistle he pushes off the back foot. The first step goes forward and low, about the length of his own foot, not up in the air.

*Start from a walk.* He walks at the cone and takes off the moment his front foot passes it. He never stops walking to start running, which is what happens in a match.

Six boys on the cones, one whistle, all six run the 15 metres and jog back. Three waves standing, three waves walking, then keep swapping. Six or seven runs each.

Nobody sprints flat out yet. Demonstrate it slowly yourself before the first wave.

**8 to 13, body shape: the sit.**

Every boy on his own disc, an arm's length apart, facing you, and nobody moves off his disc. The sit is a squat, and it is the first of the seven shapes they learn by name this year.

Say these out loud as they do it:

1. "Feet as wide as your shoulders, toes pointing forward."
2. "Arms straight out in front, just for balance."
3. "Sit down slowly, like there is a chair behind you. Count to three."
4. "Stop when your legs are level with the ground, or as low as you can go with your heels flat."
5. "Hold it. One, two, three, four, five."
6. "Stand up slowly. Count to three again."

Ten of them. Slow both ways, one thing to think about between each.

Finish by holding it for ten seconds while you walk round trying to make them laugh.

**13 to 17, challenge game: race off the ground.**

Pairs of roughly the same size, one pair per lane. Both lie flat on their fronts on the start line, a metre apart, hands under their shoulders, facing down the lane. On the whistle they get up and race ten metres.

Six or seven races, new partner each time. Nothing written down.

Rules, the same every night:

- Nobody starts before the whistle, and one coach whistles for all six lanes.
- A boy who has run jogs back up the outside of his lane.
- Every boy stays on his own disc for the shapes.
- Same station for all three groups. Set it up once, run it three times.

## Progression

Work down this and stop wherever the group is. Getting to the bottom is not the aim.

1. Standing start, walking the first two steps only. Six goes. This is where they feel the low first step.
2. Standing start into a slow ten metre run. Six goes.
3. Start from a walk into a slow ten metre run. Six goes.
4. The full 15 metres, faster, once the lanes are running clean.

## Coaching points

- **Coach one thing tonight: knees pointing the same way as the toes.** Watch the sit from the front. If the knees fall inwards, "push your knees out over your toes". Three boys properly, not 25 badly.
- The first step goes forward and low, not up. Stand at the side of a lane to see it, not at the end.
- Heels flat in the sit. If they lift, he has gone too low. Tell him to stop higher.
- Under thirty seconds of talking. Show it once, send them, explain the next bit while they move.

## If it is not working

- **Running late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes.
- **More than four waiting in a lane.** Add a seventh lane. Boys in a queue learn nothing.
- **It looks bad.** Go back a step and slow it down.
- **Lashing rain.** Only the challenge game needs the ground. Start it from a crouch, both hands on the grass.
