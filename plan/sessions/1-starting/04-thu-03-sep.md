# Session 4, Thursday 3 September

| | |
| --- | --- |

| Night | Ease off night. Same movement at match pace, then send them home fresher |
| Theme | Starting |
| Body shape | The sit |
| Next match | Hurling, Saturday 5 September |

Match on Saturday, so the four starts they know, with a hurl, at match pace.

## On the ground

- Twelve cones, six lanes, 15 metres long
- 25 marker discs beside the lanes, one per boy
- A hurl each

![Six lanes 15 metres long, a cone at each end, and a marker disc for each boy beside the lanes](../diagrams/lanes-15m.svg)

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

## Splitting them up

- Four boys to a lane, one behind the other behind the start cone. Spare boys go to the shortest lane.
- The boy at the front of each lane runs. Six lanes, so six boys go together on your whistle. That is what a wave means below.
- After his run he jogs back up the outside of his lane and joins the back of it. He is up again in about twenty seconds.

## Structure and rules

**0 to 2, wake up game: sprint to the nearest cone.**

They jog about inside the grid, hands empty. On "cone", every boy sprints to the nearest of the twelve cones and stops dead beside it, then back to jogging. Four or five goes.

**2 to 9, movement: the four starts, with a hurl.**

He carries the hurl in the hand he carries it in a match, down by his side, not across his body. The free arm keeps swinging. Out of the crouch and the kneeling start he holds the hurl in one hand and puts the other hand on the grass.

One wave at each of the four starts at half pace to get used to the weight, then the same four again at match pace.

**9 to 15, body shape: the sit.**

The sit with the hurl lying across the back of his shoulders, one hand on each end.

Sit and stand ten times, three seconds down, three seconds up. The hurl should stay level all the way. If one end dips he is leaning.

**15 to 17, finish.**

No challenge game tonight. Walk them in, ask two or three of them to name the shape, and hand them to the next station on time.

Rules, the same every night:

- Nobody starts before the whistle, and one coach whistles for all six lanes.
- A boy who has run jogs back up the outside of his lane.
- Every boy stays on his own disc for the shapes.
- Same station for all three groups. Set it up once, run it three times.

## Progression

Work down this and stop wherever the group is. Getting to the bottom is not the aim.

1. Each of the four starts once, hurl in hand, half pace.
2. The same four at match pace, the full 15 metres.
3. Two more goes at whichever start looked worst.

## Coaching points

- **Coach one thing tonight: a hurl in one hand does not stop the other arm working.** The free arm drives the same as always.
- Slow on the way down in the sit. The way down is the part that teaches it.
- Ease off night. Match in two days.

## If it is not working

- **Running late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes.
- **More than four waiting in a lane.** Add a seventh lane. Boys in a queue learn nothing.
- **It looks bad.** Go back a step and slow it down.
- **Lashing rain.** Nothing tonight needs the ground. Wet hurls are slippy, so remind them to check their grip before each wave.
