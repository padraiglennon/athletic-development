# The five autumn themes

Every theme is four sessions: two Tuesdays and two Thursdays. Tuesday teaches
and pushes. Thursday uses it at match pace and eases off before Saturday.

## 1-starting · Starting

Get out of any position fast, with the first step going forward.

## 2-endurance · Endurance

Keep running at a pace he can hold, and know what that pace feels like.

## 3-sprints · Sprints

Run fast and tall, with the arms driving front to back.

## 4-stopping · Stopping

Stop dead in two steps without falling forward. The theme that stops injuries.

## 5-jumping · Jumping

Land quietly, with the knees bent and the knees apart.
