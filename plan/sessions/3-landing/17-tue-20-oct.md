# Session 17, Tuesday 20 October

| Next match | Football, Saturday 24 October |

New theme, so teach it before you push it. No hurdles tonight: nothing gets jumped over until the landing is quiet on the spot.

## On the ground

- Twelve cones to box off the area
- 25 marker discs, one per boy, an arm's length apart
- Hurls for the body shape only
- No hurdles tonight

![Twenty five marker discs spread out an arm's length apart, no lanes](../diagrams/spots.svg)

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

## Splitting them up

- Every boy on his own disc, an arm's length from the next boy, all facing you.
- Nobody moves off his disc. That is the whole crowd control tonight, and it means all 25 are jumping at once instead of queuing.
- Everyone jumps on the same whistle so you can hear who is landing badly.

## Structure and rules

**0 to 2, wake up game: silent bunny hops.**

Two feet together, small hops around the grid, landing silent every time. Anyone you hear stands beside you for ten seconds and then goes back in. Two minutes of it.

**2 to 8, movement: land silent, then freeze.**

Everything tonight happens on their own discs.

*Jump on the spot.* Small jump straight up off two feet, land on the same disc, and freeze completely still for two seconds. Eight of them.

*Small jump forward.* The same, but landing a step in front of the disc. Eight of them.

Then a bit further, but only as far as he can still land silent. The moment you can hear him, he has gone too far and goes back.

Noise is the whole measure tonight. A quiet landing means the knees and hips are bending and taking the load. A loud one means stiff legs, and stiff legs are how ankles and knees get hurt.

**8 to 13, body shape: the landing.**

The landing is the third shape. Say these out loud as they do it:

1. "Small jump straight up off two feet."
2. "Land on the front of your feet first, then let your heels come down."
3. "Bend your knees as you land, the same as the sit."
4. "Knees out over your toes, not falling in towards each other."
5. "Freeze. One, two."

Ten of them. Then walk round and gently nudge a shoulder while they hold the landing. If he wobbles, he was not really holding it.

**13 to 17, challenge game: longest silent jump.**

Both feet together behind the line, jump forward as far as he can, and it only counts if he lands silent and holds still for three seconds.

Three goes each in pairs, one boy watching and listening while the other jumps. A noisy landing scores nothing, so the loud ones fix themselves.

Rules, the same every night:

- Nobody jumps before the whistle. Everyone goes together so you can hear the landings.
- Every boy stays on his own disc, tonight and for the shapes on any night.
- Same station for all three groups. Set it up once, run it three times.

## Progression

Work down this and stop wherever the group is. Getting to the bottom is not the aim.

1. Jump on the spot, land silent, freeze two seconds. Eight goes.
2. Small jump forward, same landing, same freeze. Eight goes.
3. A little further each time, and only as far as he can still land silent.

## Coaching points

- **Coach one thing tonight: noise.** If you can hear him land, he landed badly. Say it once, then just point at the loud ones.
- The freeze is not optional. A landing he cannot hold was too big a jump.
- Teaching night. Nothing tonight needs to be fast or far.

## If it is not working

- **Running late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes.
- **They are bunched together.** Spread the discs wider before the next group. They need room to land without touching anybody.
- **It looks bad.** Go back a step and slow it down.
- **Lashing rain.** Wet grass is fine for jumping on the spot. Keep the jumps small so nobody slides on landing.
