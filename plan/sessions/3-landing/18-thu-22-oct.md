Match on Saturday. The hurdles come out tonight, because the landing was quiet on the spot on Tuesday.

## On the ground

- Twelve cones, six lanes, 15 metres long
- Six mini hurdles, 15 cm, one per lane
- 25 marker discs beside the lanes, one per boy
- Six footballs, one per lane

![Six lanes with a mini hurdle in the middle of each, seven metres in and seven metres out, and a marker disc for each boy](../diagrams/hurdles.svg)

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

## Splitting them up

- Four boys to a lane, one behind the other behind the start cone. Spare boys go to the shortest lane.
- The boy at the front of each lane goes. Six lanes, so six boys go together on your whistle. That is what a wave means below.
- The hurdle sits in the middle of the lane. He goes in, jumps it, lands, and holds still until you say go. Nobody runs on through.
- After his go he jogs back up the outside of his lane and joins the back of it.

## Structure and rules

**0 to 2, wake up game: silent bunny hops.**

Two feet together, small hops around the grid, landing silent every time. Anyone you hear stands beside you for ten seconds and then goes back in. Two minutes of it.

**2 to 9, movement: over the hurdle, two feet to two feet.**

He walks or jogs in, jumps the hurdle off two feet, lands on two feet, and holds still for two seconds before jogging on.

Start by stepping over it and holding the landing, four waves, so they get the feel of stopping dead after the hurdle. Then jump it properly, six waves. Then the same with a ball in two hands, four waves.

Hurdles stay at 15 cm all block. There is nothing to be gained from a higher one at this age.

**9 to 15, body shape: the landing.**

The landing held for five seconds instead of two, with a ball in two hands at his chest.

Ten of them. Holding it longer is what shows you the knees: a boy who lets them fall inwards cannot hold it for five seconds without shifting.

**15 to 17, finish.**

No challenge game tonight. Walk them in, ask two or three of them to name the shape, and hand them to the next station on time.

Rules, the same every night:

- Nobody starts before the whistle, and one coach whistles for all six lanes.
- A boy who has run jogs back up the outside of his lane.
- Every boy stays on his own disc for the shapes.
- Same station for all three groups. Set it up once, run it three times.

## Progression

Work down this and stop wherever the group is. Getting to the bottom is not the aim.

1. Step over the hurdle and hold the landing. Four goes.
2. Jump the hurdle, two feet to two feet, land silent, hold two seconds. Six goes.
3. The same with a ball in two hands. Four goes.

## Coaching points

- **Coach one thing tonight: knees falling inwards on the landing.** Stand at the end of a lane and watch them come at you.
- Still no noise. The hurdle changes nothing about that.
- Ease off night. Match in two days.

## If it is not working

- **Running late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes.
- **More than four waiting in a lane.** Add a seventh lane. Boys in a queue learn nothing.
- **It looks bad.** Go back a step and slow it down.
- **Lashing rain.** Move the hurdles onto the flattest ground you have and keep them walking in rather than running.
