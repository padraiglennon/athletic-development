# Session 19, Tuesday 27 October

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Code | Football |
| Night | Push night. Teach it slowly, then put the pace up |
| Theme | Landing |
| Body shape | The landing |
| Next match | Hurling, Saturday 31 October |

## On the ground

- Twelve cones, six lanes, 15 metres long
- Six mini hurdles, 15 cm, one per lane
- 25 marker discs beside the lanes, one per boy
- No balls tonight, hands free

![Six lanes with a mini hurdle in the middle of each, seven metres in and seven metres out, and a marker disc for each boy](../diagrams/hurdles.svg)

Set it up before the first group arrives and leave it for all three.

## Splitting them up

- Four boys to a lane, one behind the other behind the start cone. Spare boys go to the shortest lane.
- The boy at the front of each lane goes. Six lanes, so six boys go together on your whistle. That is what a wave means below.
- The hurdle sits in the middle of the lane. He goes in, jumps it, lands, and holds still until you say go. Nobody runs on through.
- After his go he jogs back up the outside of his lane and joins the back of it.

## Structure and rules

**0 to 2, wake up game: silent bunny hops.**

Two feet together, small hops around the grid, landing silent every time. Anyone you hear stands beside you for ten seconds and then goes back in. Two minutes of it.

**2 to 8, movement: one foot landings.**

Tonight is the first time they land on one foot, which is what actually happens in a match and where knees get hurt. So it is slow, and every landing gets held.

*One foot to two feet.* He hops off one foot over the hurdle and lands on two. Five off each foot.

*Two feet to one foot.* He jumps off two feet and lands on one, holding it for three seconds. Five onto each foot.

The hold is how you judge it. If he cannot hold the landing still for three seconds, it was too much, and he goes back to two feet to two feet for the rest of the night.

**8 to 13, body shape: the landing.**

One foot landings on the spot. A small hop on his disc, landing on one foot, held for three seconds.

Five each leg. Knee bent, knee out over the toes, hips level. This is the hardest shape of the year so far and they will find it hard, which is the point.

**13 to 17, challenge game: longest silent jump.**

Both feet together behind the line, jump forward as far as he can, and it only counts if he lands silent and holds still for three seconds.

Three goes each in pairs, one boy watching and listening while the other jumps. A noisy landing scores nothing, so the loud ones fix themselves.

Rules, the same every night:

- Nobody starts before the whistle, and one coach whistles for all six lanes.
- A boy who has run jogs back up the outside of his lane.
- Every boy stays on his own disc for the shapes.
- Same station for all three groups. Set it up once, run it three times.

## Progression

Work down this and stop wherever the group is. Getting to the bottom is not the aim.

1. One foot to two feet over the hurdle, hold three seconds. Five off each foot.
2. Two feet to one foot, hold three seconds. Five onto each foot.
3. Anyone whose knee falls inwards goes back to two feet to two feet.

## Coaching points

- **Coach one thing tonight: where the knee goes on a one foot landing.** Watch closely and put anyone who looks bad back onto two feet.
- Everything slow. There is no reason at all to rush a landing.
- Push night, but tonight the pushing is in the difficulty, not the pace.

## If it is not working

- **Running late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes.
- **More than four waiting in a lane.** Add a seventh lane. Boys in a queue learn nothing.
- **It looks bad.** Go back a step and slow it down.
- **Lashing rain.** Drop the one foot landings entirely and repeat Thursday's two feet work. Landing on one foot on greasy grass is not worth the risk.
