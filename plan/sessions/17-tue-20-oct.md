New theme, so teach it before you push it. No hurdles tonight. Nothing gets jumped over until the landing is quiet on the spot.

## On the ground

- Twelve cones to box off the area
- 25 marker discs, one per boy, an arm's length apart
- Hurls for the body shape work only
- No hurdles tonight

```
  no lanes tonight. one disc each, an arm's length apart

     .   .   .   .   .   .   .   .
     .   .   .   .   .   .   .   .
     .   .   .   .   .   .   .   .
     .   .   .

  jump, land on the disc, freeze
```

## Structure and rules

- **0 to 2, wake up game.** Bunny hops around the grid, silent landings only. Anyone you hear is out for ten seconds.
- **2 to 8, movement.** Jump on the spot, land silent, freeze for two seconds. Then a small jump forward and the same freeze.
- **8 to 13, body shape.** The landing. Jump from his own disc, land, freeze. Walk round and gently nudge shoulders. If he wobbles he was not really holding it.
- **13 to 17, challenge game.** Longest silent jump. Two feet to two feet, and it only counts if he lands silent and holds still for three seconds.

Rules, the same every night:

- Everyone works on his own disc and nobody moves off it. That is what keeps 25 boys apart.
- One coach whistles, and everyone jumps on the same whistle.
- Every boy keeps the same disc all night.
- Same station for all three groups. Set it up once, run it three times.

## Progression

Work down this list and stop wherever the group is. Getting to the bottom of it is not the aim.

1. Jump on the spot, land silent, freeze two seconds. Eight goes.
2. Small jump forward, same landing, same freeze. Eight goes.
3. The same again a little further, and only as far as he can still land silent.

## Coaching points

- **Coach one thing tonight: noise.** If you can hear him land, he landed badly. Say it once, then just keep pointing at the loud ones.
- The freeze is two seconds and it is not optional. A landing he cannot hold was too big a jump.
- Teaching night. Nothing here needs to be fast.

## If it is not working

- **Running late.** Cut the wake up game. Never cut the body shapes.
- **It looks bad.** Go back a step in the progression. Slower and correct beats faster and messy.
- **Lashing rain.** Nothing here needs the ground except lying and kneeling. The swaps are at the end of the [activity bank](../activities.md#wet-night-alternatives).
