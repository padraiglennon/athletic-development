# Kit list, 100 euro

The athletic development station only ever has one group at it, about 25 boys, so you only need to kit out 25 boys, not 80. That is what makes 100 euro enough.

## Buy this

| What | How many | Roughly | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flat marker discs, mixed colours | 50 | 20 euro | Every boy gets his own spot. Grids, lanes, targets. The single most useful thing on this list |
| Small cones, 15 cm | 20 | 10 euro | Lane ends, gates, turning points |
| Mini hurdles, 15 cm | 6 | 18 euro | Landing and jumping. Low ones only |
| Bibs | 12 | 18 euro | Splitting the group, tag games, tucking in the waistband as tails |
| Tennis balls | 12 | 8 euro | Reacting, catching, chasing |
| Foam balls | 4 | 12 euro | Tag games and throwing where a real ball would hurt |
| Big bucket or bag | 1 | 9 euro | Everything lives in it, so nothing goes missing |
| Left over | | 5 euro | Something will break |

That comes to 100 euro.

## If the club already has cones and bibs

Most clubs do. If so, spend the money on these instead:

| What | How many | Roughly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More mini hurdles | 6 more | 18 euro |
| A reaction ball, the odd-shaped one that bounces sideways | 2 | 16 euro |
| Coloured wristbands or ribbons for tag games | 24 | 12 euro |
| A second set of 50 discs in different colours | 50 | 20 euro |

## What not to buy

**Speed ladders.** They look the part and every club has one. The problem is that one boy uses it while twenty four stand watching, and what he practises is stepping neatly between rungs, which is not a thing that happens in a match. Spend the money on discs.

**Resistance bands, weighted vests, parachutes, anything that adds weight.** Not at this age. Nothing heavier than the boy himself.

**A stopwatch.** We are not timing anyone.

## What the autumn themes need

Nothing on the list above is bought for one theme only, and no theme needs anything that is not on it.

| Theme | The only thing worth checking you have |
| --- | --- |
| Starting | 12 cones and 25 discs |
| Endurance | 10 cones for the loop, and 12 tennis balls for the wake up game |
| Sprints | 16 cones, because two gates go out as well as the lanes |
| Stopping | 18 cones, because every lane needs a stop line as well as two ends |
| Jumping | 6 mini hurdles, 15 cm. Low ones only, and nothing taller ever |

Bibs are used as tags and as tails. Twelve is enough, because the tail game runs with twelve boys wearing one and the rest chasing.

## Setting up

Put everything down before the boys arrive, and leave it there for all three groups. You set the station up once and run it three times. That is worth about five minutes of coaching time per group, which at this station is a third of what you have.
