# The activity bank

A bank of themes. A theme lasts four sessions: two Tuesdays and two Thursdays. What order the themes run in is in the [year plan](year-plan.md), and the autumn order is in [autumn-2026.md](autumn-2026.md).

Every theme has the same three parts, so once the boys know how the station runs you stop having to explain it: a wake up game of 2 minutes, a movement of 8 minutes, and a challenge game of 7 minutes.

Four sessions is two turns of the fixture cycle, so every theme gets two hurling nights and two football nights. Tuesday is for teaching it slowly and pushing it. Thursday is for using it at speed and easing off before the match. The activity is the same both nights. The pace is not. See [Tuesday and Thursday](session-guide.md#tuesday-and-thursday).

The wake up game is the one part that changes every night. Twenty different games are written out in the autumn run sheets in [sessions](sessions/). Use them again in any theme.

Kit is from the [kit list](equipment.md). The grid is in the [session guide](session-guide.md).

## The seven body shapes

The body shapes are a theme of their own, and that theme starts in November. They are not a part of every night. The table is here so the shapes theme has one place to work from.

The boys should end up able to name them and do them without being shown.

| Name | What it is | What you are looking for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The sit | Squat down, heels flat, chest up | Knees pointing the same way as the toes |
| The bow | Bend forward from the hips, back flat, knees soft | The back stays flat, the bend comes from the hips |
| The big step | Long step forward, back knee down towards the ground | Front knee stays over the front foot, not falling inwards |
| The press | The top of a press-up, straight from head to heels | No sagging in the middle |
| The pull | Face a partner, hold hands, both lean back and pull | Body stays straight, no bending at the waist |
| The plank | On elbows and toes, straight as a board | Backside down, not up in the air |
| The landing | Land from a jump, knees bent, absolutely silent | No noise, and the knees do not fall inwards |

## Theme 1: starting

Four run sheets: [sessions 1 to 4](sessions/1-starting/).

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Everyone jogs in the area. On the shout, sprint to the nearest cone and stop. Repeat.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** First step. Waves of six, 15 metres. Every run starts differently. Standing still, from a jog, sitting on the ground, lying on their front, facing backwards, on one knee. Six runs, six different starts.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Pairs, side by side, one metre apart, both lying on their fronts. On the shout, both get up and race ten metres.

**Watch for.** The first step should go forward, not up. Boys who bounce upright first are wasting a step.

**Easier.** Start standing every time.
**Harder.** Start with eyes shut and go on the shout.

## Theme 2: endurance

Four run sheets: [sessions 5 to 8](sessions/2-endurance/).

This is not fitness work and it is not a race. The match rotation makes them fit. This theme teaches one thing: a pace a boy can hold, and how to tell when he is above it.

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Everybody moving inside a loop, and nobody stops for the whole two minutes.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** The loop, about 70 metres round, all 25 running the same way at once. Ninety seconds on, thirty seconds walking, two or three goes. Slower boys cut the corners inside so nobody gets lapped.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Two minutes of running with every boy counting his own laps. The group adds them up, and that number is the target for the next Tuesday.

**The talking test.** Run beside a boy and ask him his name and his club. A full sentence means he is too easy. Short words mean he is in the right band. No answer means he is too hard.

**Watch for.** Boys who sprint the first lap and walk the third. That is the whole fault of this theme.

**Easier.** Sixty seconds instead of ninety, and a shorter loop.
**Harder.** Shuttles out to a called cone at 5, 10 or 15 metres, continuous for sixty seconds.

## Theme 3: sprints

Four run sheets: [sessions 9 to 12](sessions/3-sprints/).

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Everybody jogging, and a short sprint on every shout.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Running tall. Waves of six, 15 metres at about three quarter pace for the first two nights and flat out on the third. Arms driving front to back, not across the body. Head still. Tall through the middle.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Pair races over 15 metres. Later in the theme, two gates 12 metres away and the coach calls which gate as the boy is already running.

**Watch for.** Arms swinging across the chest, and heads rolling side to side.

**Easier.** Shorter distance, slower, and no gates.
**Harder.** Run with a hurl in one hand and keep the other arm working properly.

---

## Theme 4: stopping

Four run sheets: [sessions 13 to 16](sessions/4-stopping/).

The most important theme in the whole bank. Most juvenile injuries come from stopping badly, and nobody teaches it.

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Traffic lights. Green is run, red is stop dead. Anyone still moving does ten fast steps on the spot.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Stopping. Waves of six. Run and stop on a line. Start at a jog, build to a run over the six goes. The rule is two steps to stop, no more, and no falling forward.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Stop on the number. Coach holds up fingers as they run. They stop at the line and shout the number. Now they have to look up while stopping, which is what happens in a match.

**Watch for.** Stopping upright with straight legs. It should look like sitting down into the stop.

**Easier.** Walk it, then jog it.
**Harder.** Stop on one foot.

## Theme 5: jumping

Four run sheets: [sessions 17 to 20](sessions/5-jumping/).

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Bunny hops around the grid, silent landings only. Anyone the coach hears is out for ten seconds.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Jumping and landing. Six lanes, one mini hurdle each. Jump over, land silent, hold it for two seconds. Two feet to two feet, then one foot to two feet, then two feet to one foot.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Longest silent jump. Two feet to two feet, must land and hold still for three seconds or it does not count.

**Watch for.** Noise, and knees falling inwards. Those two things are the whole theme.

**Easier.** No hurdle, just jump on the spot.
**Harder.** Jump, turn 90 degrees in the air, land silent.

---

## Theme 6: turning

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Follow the leader in threes around the grid, leader changes direction whenever he likes.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Turning. Waves of six. Run 10 metres out, turn all the way round, run back. Turn off the right foot three times, then the left three times. Most boys have a bad side and have never noticed.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Chase in pairs. One runs, one follows, out and back with a turn. The follower is trying to stay within touching distance.

**Watch for.** Boys running a big banana shape instead of turning. Put a cone down and make them go round it tight.

**Easier.** Walk the turn.
**Harder.** Turn on a shout, so they do not know which end they are turning at.

---

## Theme 7: sidestepping

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Tag in a small square, no running through the middle allowed, so they have to go round and change direction.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Sidestepping. Waves of six through a gate of cones. Run, plant the outside foot, push off sideways, go. Three off the right foot, three off the left.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Beat the defender. One boy walks forward as a defender, the runner has to get past him with a sidestep. Defender is not allowed to run. Or run [the gauntlet](#the-gauntlet), which is the same idea with more boys moving at once.

**Watch for.** Boys who slow to a walk before the sidestep. The whole point is doing it at speed.

**Easier.** Cones only, no defender.
**Harder.** Defender allowed to move at half pace.

---

## Theme 8: backwards and sideways

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Everyone moving in the grid. Coach calls forwards, backwards, sideways left, sideways right.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Waves of six. Ten metres backwards, then turn and ten metres forwards. Then ten metres sideways, then turn and sprint. Both directions.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Mirror game in pairs. One boy moves sideways along a line, the other mirrors him. Ten seconds, then swap. Or run [the gauntlet](#the-gauntlet), where the defending job is this movement for two minutes solid.

**Watch for.** Crossing the feet when moving sideways. Feet should not touch.

**Easier.** Slower, shorter.
**Harder.** Mirror game where the leader can go backwards and forwards too.

---

## Theme 9: doing it with a hurl or a ball

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Jog with a ball in hand, coach calls stop, turn or jump, and they do it with the ball still in hand.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Everything from themes 1 to 8, but with a hurl or a ball in hand. Start with a ball, stop with a ball, turn with a hurl. This is where the station connects to the skills station.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** Run, stop, turn and pass. In pairs, ten metres apart.

**Watch for.** Movement falling apart the second there is a ball involved. That is normal and it is exactly why this theme exists.

**Easier.** Ball in two hands.
**Harder.** Hurl in one hand, ball in the other.

---

## Theme 10: joining it up

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Free running with the coach calling stop, turn, go.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Chains of three. Run and stop, then turn, then sprint. Or run, sidestep, sprint. Or jump, land, sprint. Six lanes, waves of six.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** An obstacle course using the whole grid. Every movement from the year in it once. Run it in teams.

**Watch for.** The join between two movements. That is where it falls apart, and it is where the match is won.

**Easier.** Chains of two.
**Harder.** Coach calls the chain out only as they set off.

---

## Theme 11: reacting

The last theme of the year and the closest to a real match.

**Wake up game, 2 minutes.** Everyone jogging. Coach points a direction, everyone sprints that way for three seconds.

**Movement, 8 minutes.** Waves of six, but nobody knows what is coming. Coach shouts left, right, stop, back, go, while they are running.


**Challenge game, 7 minutes.** One against one in a small square. Attacker tries to get to either of two gates, defender tries to stop him. Ten seconds, then swap. Everything from the year is in this. Or run [the gauntlet](#the-gauntlet) at full tilt, with a ball in hand.

**Watch for.** Head up. If he is watching his feet he cannot react to anything.

**Easier.** Coach signals early.
**Harder.** Two things to react to at once.

## The gauntlet

From the pack the club sports director sent, originally an LTAD Network card. See [the notes on it](../knowledge/sample-exercises-notes.md). It replaces the challenge game in themes 7, 8 and 11, and it is worth the swap.

An attacker has to get from one end of a channel to the other without being tagged. Defenders stand on lines across the channel and may only move sideways along their own line.

**Setting it up.** Three channels side by side, about 12 metres long and 8 wide, marked with discs. Four lines across each channel with a defender on each. Eight boys to a channel, so four defending and four running.

**Running it.** Send an attacker every few seconds so there is always somebody in the channel. Two minutes, then the defenders and the attackers swap over.

**Why it works with 25 boys.** The defenders are getting the session too. Shuffling sideways along a line is the movement we are after, so nobody in the channel is standing still. That is the only reason a one at a time game earns a place at this station.

**Watch for.** Defenders crossing their feet, and attackers slowing to a walk before they try to beat someone.

**Easier.** Wider channel, three defenders instead of four, and no crossovers, shuffling only.
**Harder.** Ball in hand. Two attackers going together so one can draw a defender out. Defenders allowed to change direction anywhere along their line instead of only at the ends.

## Wet night alternatives

Everything works in the wet except lying or kneeling on the ground. Swap those for standing versions:

- The press becomes a press against a wall or a partner's hands.
- The plank becomes the sit, held for longer.
- Starts from lying down become starts from a crouch.
