## Worth adding later

Referenced by the action statement and directly relevant:

- Gaelic Games Player Pathway Guide (Dempsey, Flanagan and Cuthbert, 2020), https://learning.gaa.ie
- Duggan et al., Considerations and Guidelines on Athletic Development for Youth Gaelic Athletic Association Players, Strength and Conditioning Journal 44(2), 2022. The closest thing to a practical companion for this age group.
- Lloyd and Oliver, The Youth Physical Development Model, Strength and Conditioning Journal 34(3), 2012. The model behind the age-related recommendations.
- GAA15 warm-up. Shown to reduce lower limb injury in adolescent hurlers (Kelly and Lodge, 2018).
